Privacy Policy

FileToWhere is designed around browser-based file processing. Where supported, files are processed directly on your device instead of being uploaded to FileToWhere.

Your files

FileToWhere does not intentionally store files that are processed entirely inside your browser.

Third-party software

Some tools may load browser libraries from third-party content delivery networks. Those providers may receive ordinary technical request information such as IP address, browser type and request time.

Analytics and advertising

FileToWhere may use privacy-conscious analytics and may introduce advertising or other monetization services. This policy will be updated when necessary.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the Contact page.
